About ServiceNow Learning Hub

ServiceNow Learning Hub is an independent educational resource for learners and IT professionals exploring ServiceNow platform concepts, IT service management, IT operations, employee workflows, automation and AI-related capabilities.

Our editorial approach

  • Explain technical concepts in practical, beginner-friendly language.
  • Separate general learning guidance from official product documentation and certification requirements.
  • Update articles when meaningful changes are made and keep article dates transparent.
  • Avoid promises about rankings, certification success, salary or job placement.

Official documentation

For product-specific details, supported features and current certification requirements, confirm information in the official ServiceNow documentation.

Relationship disclosure

This site is not operated by or affiliated with ServiceNow. Some learning resources may link to TechPratham course information. Such links are provided for readers who want to explore structured training; they do not replace official product documentation.
