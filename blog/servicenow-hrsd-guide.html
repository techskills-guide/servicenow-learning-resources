ServiceNow HRSD: Modules, Employee Workflows and Skills

Human Resources Service Delivery (HRSD) helps organisations manage employee questions and HR services through structured digital workflows. Rather than relying on scattered emails, HR teams can use consistent case handling, knowledge resources and employee-facing service experiences.

What is HRSD?

HRSD is an employee-service approach built around making HR help easier to request, route, track and resolve. Implementation choices depend on organisational processes, data access rules and the ServiceNow capabilities licensed and configured.

Important HRSD concepts

HR case management

HR cases provide a structured way to capture an employee question, route it to an appropriate HR team, track progress and document resolution. Good case design includes clear categories, ownership, service targets and appropriate access controls.

HR knowledge

Knowledge articles can help employees find answers to common questions without opening a case. Articles should be reviewed regularly, use plain language and respect audience-specific access permissions.

Employee service experience

An employee portal or service experience brings HR information and requests into a more consistent entry point. A useful design considers mobile access, search quality, accessibility and the employee's language.

Employee lifecycle workflows

Lifecycle workflows can coordinate steps associated with events such as onboarding or offboarding. These processes often involve multiple teams, approvals, equipment and access tasks, making ownership and auditability important.

Benefits and practical considerations

  • More consistent routing and tracking of HR requests.
  • Improved visibility into case volumes and recurring questions.
  • Better access to approved self-service information.
  • More coordinated tasks across HR and supporting departments.

These benefits depend on thoughtful process design and data quality. HR records may contain sensitive information, so role-based access, retention practices and testing are critical.

Skills for a beginner

  1. Learn ServiceNow navigation, tables, forms, lists and access controls.
  2. Understand case lifecycle, assignment, categorisation and knowledge management.
  3. Practise designing a simple request flow with sample data.
  4. Learn how to test permissions for different user roles.
  5. Document a workflow, its exceptions and how success would be measured.

Project idea: employee address-change request

Create a fictional request journey with an employee submission, validation step, assignment to the right team and a completion notification. Define who can see each field, what happens if information is missing and how the requester checks status. Use synthetic data only.
