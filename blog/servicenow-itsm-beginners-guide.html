ServiceNow ITSM for Beginners: Incidents, Problems and Changes

IT Service Management (ITSM) is the practice of organising technology services around user needs, reliable processes and measurable outcomes. ServiceNow can support these processes with records, assignment rules, approvals, knowledge and reporting.

Incident management

An incident is an unplanned interruption or reduction in the quality of a service. Incident management aims to restore service quickly. A typical record captures the issue, impact, urgency, priority, assignment and resolution details.

Problem management

Problem management looks for the underlying causes of one or more incidents. Teams may investigate recurring patterns, document workarounds and track corrective actions to reduce future disruption.

Change management

Change management provides a controlled way to assess and coordinate modifications to services or infrastructure. The level of review should reflect risk, impact and organisational policy. A change record can document scope, implementation steps, testing, approvals and a back-out plan.

Requests and knowledge

A service request is a request for a standard service or item, such as access or software. Knowledge articles help users solve common issues and help support agents deliver consistent answers. Requests and incidents are related but should not be treated as the same process.

How these processes work together

Imagine multiple users report that an internal application is unavailable. Support teams log and prioritise incidents, investigate whether a common problem exists, and coordinate a change if a fix is required. A knowledge article may document a safe workaround while the permanent fix is prepared.

Beginner practice checklist

  • Learn the difference between an incident, problem, request and change.
  • Practise capturing complete records using fictional scenarios.
  • Understand assignment groups, priorities, status transitions and approvals.
  • Explore reports for trends, response times and recurring issues.
  • Review role permissions and avoid using real personal or business data in practice.

Build a small portfolio example

Document a sample outage from report to resolution. Include a fictional incident, a short root-cause analysis, a proposed change with testing and rollback steps, and a knowledge note for support staff. Explain why each record exists and what evidence would show the process worked.
