START HERE

A simple path for learning ServiceNow

Build understanding step by step instead of trying to learn every module at once.

Step 1

Understand the platform and ITSM

Start with records, tables, forms, users, roles, incidents, requests and change processes. Learn the purpose of each workflow before moving into advanced configuration.

Step 2

Practise configuration and workflows

Use an authorized practice environment to test form behaviour, access rules and simple automations. Document the requirement, expected result and test cases.

Step 3

Choose a role and build evidence

Compare administrator, developer and analyst responsibilities, complete a small project and explain your decisions clearly. Use the ServiceNow career roadmap to plan your next steps.

When evaluating a structured course, use the course comparison checklist and verify current platform or certification details through official resources.
